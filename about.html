About

Hi! I'm Moss Wilder, though some of you may know me by the name on my library card, Michelle Baker. I'm a professional artist, illustrator and poet with more than fifteen years of experience across digital and physical media.

I move between digital and physical spaces to create something unique and I love experimenting with new ways of doing things, showing the steps in between: the rough concepts, the wrong turns and the finished piece.

When I'm not covered in paint and pixels, you can find me creating chaos in the kitchen and garden, reading a story for new inspiration, or attempting to learn the rules for a new board game or TTRPG.
